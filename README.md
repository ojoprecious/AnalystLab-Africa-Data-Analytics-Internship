# AnalystLab Africa – Data Analytics Internship

This repository holds my weekly internship projects. Each week builds on the one before it.

## Week 1 – Telco Customer Churn Analysis
I cleaned and explored customer churn data in Python, built visualizations, and identified the customers most likely to leave.

## Week 2 – Global Superstore BI Dashboard
I cleaned the Global Superstore dataset in Power Query and built my first interactive Power BI dashboard with 5 KPIs, market/category/product analysis and slicers. Key finding: deep discounts were killing profit.

## Week 3 – Advanced Analysis, KPIs & BI Dashboard
I built a second dashboard page on top of Week 2: 7 KPIs, 13 DAX measures, time-based analysis, discount-impact analysis, loss-making products and top customers. Key finding: discounts above 40% and the Tables sub-category are the main profit leaks, while top customers achieve double the average margin.

## Week 4 – HealthConnect Clinic Experience Lab (Kickoff)

I joined the HealthConnect Clinic project as the data analyst. I reviewed the appointment dataset (5,000 records, 18 columns), ran an initial data quality assessment, defined business questions about patient no-shows, and proposed five KPIs linked to those questions. Initial observation: roughly 4 in 10 appointments end in a no-show, with long booking lead times and weak reminder coverage as the biggest risk factors.

## How to open
Download the .pbix files and open them with Power BI Desktop (free). Click the slicers to explore.
